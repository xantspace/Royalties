Admissions 2026/2027

Begin your child's journey toward greatness

Admissions are open across Pre-School, Primary and Secondary at both our Aba and Oron campuses. Here's how the process works, and what to prepare.

How Admissions Works

Four steps, start to finish

A straightforward process designed to help us understand your child and place them at the right level.

01

Submit an Enquiry

Complete the enquiry form below or contact your preferred campus directly with your child's details and intended level.

02

Campus Visit & Assessment

Visit the campus to tour the school and complete a placement assessment appropriate to your child's age and level.

03

Documentation Review

Submit required documents for review by the admissions office. Requirements to be confirmed

04

Offer & Enrollment

Once accepted, complete enrollment and fee arrangements to secure your child's place for the term.
