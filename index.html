Our Founding Story

Royalties International Christian Schools — known affectionately as Golden Royalties — was born with a singular aim: to programme pupils and students for Greatness. From the beginning, our founders understood that the word shapes the person, and that every great institution is built on language used with purpose.

Our name is intentional. A royal identity is not inherited by chance — it is formed through discipline, instruction, and an uncompromising commitment to virtue. That is the standard we hold for every student who passes through our gates.

Academic Levels

One school, three stages of formation

Each level is built on the one before it — early foundations, structured primary learning, and the discipline students carry into secondary school and beyond.

Stage One

Pre-School

Early foundations in language, numeracy, and character — a gentle, structured introduction to school life.

Stage Two

Primary

Core academic development with consistent routines, guided instruction, and growing independence.

Stage Three

Secondary

Advanced coursework and discipline-building that prepares students for examinations and life beyond school.

Why Families Choose Royalties

Rare virtues. Real character. Lasting greatness.

In a world of moral decay, we equip students with the virtues that outlast every credential — honesty, boldness, kindness, and courtesy. These qualities are not extras; they are the curriculum.

God's Word is our bond

Faith is not an elective here — it is the foundation. Our staff use language with intention, knowing that people are shaped by the words spoken over them. Kings recognise the power of language; so do we.

Programming for Greatness

Every routine, lesson, and interaction is designed to install virtues — excellence, punctuality, resilience, and boldness — that shape individuals of exceptional character.

Overcoming today's moral decay

Honesty, kindness, respect, and courtesy are countercultural in today's world. Royalties makes them non-negotiable — not through force, but through daily formation.
